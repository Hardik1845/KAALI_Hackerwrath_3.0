A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Your information stays yours.

Location

Your browser asks before sharing GPS. Routing sends start and destination coordinates to the open Valhalla service. Sampled route points and the nearby-support map area go to OpenStreetMap services for map tags. SOS adds a map link only after you ask.

Images & camera

Deepfake checks send the selected image to this computer’s local DeepDefend service over localhost. It is analyzed in memory and not saved or sent to a cloud model. Camera bursts save in this device’s browser storage. Hash Shield reference images are sent to the local service for fingerprinting. External reverse-image search sends the image to the configured provider only after you choose the non-intimate category and press the consent button.

Reports & contacts

Contacts remain in browser storage. Community notes are kept in this app’s local SQLite database and share a location rounded to about 100 metres.
